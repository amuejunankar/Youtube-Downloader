What it does

Get started in three steps

  1. Download the installer or the portable exe above.
  2. Open the app and paste a YouTube link.
  3. Pick a quality and press Download. Files land in your Downloads folder.

Questions

Windows says the app is from an unknown publisher.

The build is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn you. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can review the full source code in the repository.

Do I need to install Python or FFmpeg?

No. FFmpeg is bundled inside both builds, so merging video and audio works out of the box.

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is open source and free to use. There are no ads, accounts, or paid tiers.

Is downloading videos allowed?

Only download content you own or are authorised to download, and follow the terms of the site you use. This tool does not bypass DRM or paid content.
