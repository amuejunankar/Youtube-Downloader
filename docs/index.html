FAQ

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Both apps are free and open source, with no ads, accounts, trials, or paid tiers. You can read and build the source yourself.

Windows shows "unknown publisher". Is that safe?

The builds are not signed with a commercial code-signing certificate, so SmartScreen warns about a new app. Click More info, then Run anyway. The full source and build scripts are on GitHub.

How do I install the Android APK?

Download the APK, open it, and allow Install unknown apps for the app you downloaded it with (Chrome, Files, etc.). The app is signed and targets Android 7.0 and newer on 64-bit devices.

Do I need Python or FFmpeg?

No. FFmpeg and the download engine are bundled in both builds, so merging video and audio works immediately after install.

Where do downloads go?

On Windows they go to your Downloads folder. On Android they appear in Movies/YouTube Downloader for video and Music/YouTube Downloader for audio, indexed by the system gallery and music apps.

Why are some Android downloads MKV?

YouTube's higher resolutions often use VP9 or AV1 video with Opus audio, which cannot be merged into MP4. The app uses MKV in those cases, and players such as VLC or MX Player handle it natively.

Is downloading allowed?

Only download content you own or are authorised to download, and follow the terms of the site you use. The app does not bypass DRM, paywalls, or private content.
